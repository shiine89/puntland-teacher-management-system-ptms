Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register as a teacher?

Visit our registration page and fill out the complete form with your qualifications, experience, and documents.

What documents do I need?

You need a recent photo, your CV, and certificates of your educational qualifications.

How long does registration take?

Registration is instant. However, verification and approval may take 2-3 business days.

Can I update my information?

Yes, you can contact our support team to update your profile information at any time.
